About the project

Why road monitoring matters

Deteriorating roads cost billions annually, cause accidents, and damage vehicles. Manual inspection is slow and error-prone — AI enables continuous, scalable infrastructure monitoring across entire city networks.

Role of CNNs in computer vision

Convolutional Neural Networks learn spatial features automatically — edges, textures, and damage patterns — making them ideal for visual defect detection without hand-crafted rules. Transfer learning from ImageNet gives powerful priors with limited training data.

Industry applications

Used by municipalities for smart maintenance scheduling, insurance firms for damage assessment, autonomous vehicles for hazard avoidance, and civil engineering teams for large-scale road audit automation.
